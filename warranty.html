Warranty FAQs

Does the warranty cover accidental damage?

Standard warranties do not cover accidental damage such as drops, spills, or cracks. However, our Extended and Premium Protection Plans do include varying levels of accidental damage coverage. The Premium Protection Plan offers the most comprehensive coverage for multiple incidents.

How do I check my warranty status?

You can check your warranty status by logging into your TechNest account and viewing your registered products. Alternatively, you can enter your product's serial number on our warranty verification page or contact our customer support team with your proof of purchase.

Can I extend my warranty after purchase?

Yes, you can extend your warranty within 30 days of your original purchase date. After this period, warranty extensions may still be available but are subject to device inspection and additional terms. Visit your account dashboard or contact customer service to explore extension options.

What documents do I need for a warranty claim?

For warranty claims, you'll need your proof of purchase (receipt or order confirmation), the product's serial number, and your TechNest account information. For Premium Protection Plan claims, you may also need to provide details about the incident that caused the damage.

Will I get a replacement device during repair?

Premium Protection Plan members receive loaner devices during repair periods. Extended Warranty customers may request a loaner device subject to availability. Standard Warranty repairs do not typically include loaner devices, but expedited repair services are available.
